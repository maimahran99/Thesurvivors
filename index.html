He lost ten years.

01What kind of game is The Survivors?+

The Survivors is an interactive mystery told through Hazem’s phone. You’ll chat with characters, receive calls, uncover clues, and make choices as you investigate what really happened on December 31, 2016. The game is available now on Google Play, with iOS coming soon.

02Will the game support more languages?+

The complete story is planned across 8 episodes. Episodes 1–3 are available now, and Episode 4 is currently in development.

03How many Episodes will there be?+

The Survivors is available on Android through Google Play. The iPhone version is currently under App Store review; its download link will be added as soon as it is available.

04Is the game completely free?+

Yes. The game is completely free. There are no paid choices and no in-app purchases.
